Our Story

A Tradition Centuries in the Making

Lanzhou beef noodles — 兰州牛肉面 — originated in Lanzhou, the capital of Gansu Province in northwestern China. For over 200 years, this dish has been perfected: a clear, spiced broth simmered for hours, hand-pulled noodles crafted fresh to order, and tender braised beef sliced thin and arranged with care.

Ancient Lanzhou Beef Noodles brings this authentic tradition to Christchurch. Every recipe follows the original Lanzhou method — from the five-spice bone broth to the hand-pulled noodles, each bowl is an honest representation of one of China's most celebrated street foods.

We believe great food doesn't need shortcuts. Come taste the real thing.
